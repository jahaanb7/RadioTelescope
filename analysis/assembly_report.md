### Not checked, and why
- Rim girder, hub, spacers: they carry under 70 N in total; their sizes are set by printing and handling, not stress.
- Fatigue, temperature: the telescope is moved by hand a few times per session; PETG softens near 70 C, so do not leave it in full summer sun for hours.
- Wood moisture: sockets have 0.8 mm clearance so swelling does not split them; seal batten ends.
