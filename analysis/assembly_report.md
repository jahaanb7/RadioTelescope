### Dish joints and feed support
- Rim joint: whole dish weight shared by 36 M4 bolts gives 1.6 N per bolt; bearing in the 3.17 mm rib 0.13 MPa (plywood allowable 15). The joint is clamped, so friction carries it before the bolt bears.
- Feed head 0.75 kg on three 19 mm square legs, 504 mm between socket starts: worst leg force with a 20 N knock 24 N; Euler load 4016 N (1 % used). One M4 per leg end: 0.32 MPa wood bearing.
- Feed movement: leg stretch is under 0.01 mm. Joint clearance (0.8 mm in the sockets) lets the feed shift up to about 0.5 mm, against a focus tolerance of about lambda/10 = 21 mm.

### Not checked, and why
- Rim girder, hub, spacers: they carry under 70 N in total; their sizes are set by printing and handling, not stress.
- Fatigue, temperature: the telescope is moved by hand a few times per session; PETG softens near 70 C, so do not leave it in full summer sun for hours.
- Wood moisture: sockets have 0.8 mm clearance so swelling does not split them; seal batten ends.
